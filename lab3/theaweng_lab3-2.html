Sources and method

Tree canopy: City of Seattle, TreeCanopy_2021_Seattle (2021). Canopy polygons were styled in green and rendered into transparent PNG tiles in QGIS.

Parks: City of Seattle, Park Boundary (details). Downloaded October 25, 2026. A park may contain multiple property parcels.

Basemap: Mapbox Outdoors, adapted in Mapbox Studio and exported through QGIS. Both raster layers use local 256-pixel XYZ tiles, zoom levels 8–12.

Canopy and park data represent different dates. This map does not calculate canopy percentages or measure park accessibility.
